Block **B4** is Phase 4 of [docs/PLAN.md](https://github.com/sebastian-suarez/turning-table/blob/main/docs/PLAN.md#phases). Build order: B3 → B4 → B5 → B6 (the **Order** field on the board). The plan holds the design and its inputs (phones, object, printer); this card holds the progress: tick a step only after verifying it, and say how it was verified in a comment. The plan gives no time estimates, so the steps carry none.

**Delivers:** the printed rig: a base with the electronics bay, the platter on bearings, and the counterweighted camera arc arm driven by the second 28BYJ-48 through a printed ~8:1 reduction.
**Comes after:** B3. **Comes before:** B5.
**Done when:** the rig is assembled on the shield's 5 V with strain-relieved cables, the arm is balanced, the Phase 2 acceptance is repeated with the platter loaded, and the three ring angles are repeatable within ±0.5°.

## Steps

- [ ] Parametric OpenSCAD sources in `hardware/cad/`, STLs exported from the command line, sized for the Creality Hi (260 × 260 × 300 mm).
- [ ] Platter: 250 mm single piece (a two-segment 300 mm variant if the mirror cap needs it), hub on two stacked 608ZZ so the motor shaft carries no platter weight, marker ring on the rim.
- [ ] Arc arm: pivot on a horizontal axis at the object's centre height, radius ≈ 35 cm, elevation 5°..65° for three rings (about 10°, 35°, 60°), printed in two bolted segments.
- [ ] Pivot on an M8 partially threaded bolt in two 608ZZ bearings with printed spacers; hard stops so the arm cannot swing the phone into the platter.
- [ ] Printed ~8:1 reduction (spur pair, or a worm if self-locking matters) from the second 28BYJ-48 to the arm.
- [ ] Adjustable counterweight behind the pivot: with the motor unplugged the arm stays wherever it is put.
- [ ] Phone clamp: screw clamp for 74-80 mm width, 5-10 mm thickness and 250 g plus a case; lens on the arm's radial line; USB-C cutout.
- [ ] Electronics bay in the base for the Nano, both ULN2003 boards and the shield, with the shield's button, slide switch, LEDs and charging ports reachable from outside and the cell removable without tools.
- [ ] Index marker or switch for homing the arm, if open-loop drift shows.
- [ ] Wiring: second ULN2003 on D6, D7, D8, D10, shield 5 V with common ground, cables strain-relieved; BOM and wiring recorded in `hardware/`.
- [ ] Exit run: Phase 2 acceptance repeated with the platter loaded, and the three ring angles repeatable within ±0.5°, results pasted in a comment.
